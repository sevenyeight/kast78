Acme Tackle · Freshwater · SoCal Guide
What It Is
A heavy, solid metal spoon with a slanted body cut. That angle is everything — it gives the lure a natural wobble and flutter as it moves through the water, mimicking a wounded baitfish. Sinks fast, casts far, and works at any depth.
How to Fish It
🎯
Steady Retrieve
Cast out, let it sink 3–5 seconds, then reel at a medium pace. The lure handles the action — just keep it moving.
⚡
Vary Your Speed
Speed up suddenly, then slow down. Fish often strike right when you pause — they think the bait is trying to escape.
🌊
Let It Flutter Down
After casting, count to 5 before reeling. The falling wobble triggers strikes — count deeper if fish aren't hitting near the surface.
🎣
Rod Twitches
Flick the rod tip while reeling to add irregular darting movement. Deadly for bass and trout that want a reaction bite.

Pro Tips
01 Use a snap swivel between the line and lure — the spinning action will twist your line over time without one.
02 Don't set the hook hard. The treble is sharp and fish usually self-hook — a firm lift is enough.
03 If you're getting follows but no bites, slow way down or speed way up — the fish want a different trigger.
04 Fish it near structure — rocky points, drop-offs, submerged trees. The lure will bounce off and flutter down, which is deadly.
